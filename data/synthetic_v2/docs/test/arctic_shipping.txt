The seasonal shipping review records that sea ice delayed the eastern convoy during the first survey. A second table projects that sea ice will remain the principal delay through the next cycle. The northern route is open for only a short period, and the appendix says that the northern route requires advance weather reporting. The safest response to a late freeze would be an icebreaker escort rather than a larger cargo manifest. The report names Captain Sorensen as the officer who approved the route, but does not describe his experience. The fleet could continue if the weather window remained stable, although the estimate excludes unreported mechanical failures.
